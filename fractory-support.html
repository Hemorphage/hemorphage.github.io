Common questions

I purchased the full game but it's not unlocked. Open Fractory → Options → Restore Purchases. If you're on a new device, make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you purchased with.

Where's my Daily Challenge streak / leaderboard rank? The Daily Challenge leaderboard resets each UTC day and is tied to your device, not an account — reinstalling or switching devices starts a new history.
