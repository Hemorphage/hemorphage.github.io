24×36

This is a film camera.

A roll holds 24 or 36 photos. You can't see them until the lab sends the roll back. Film is free and unlimited. You choose the day and hour it comes back when you load it.

Contact

Write to hemorphage.dev@gmail.com. A person reads every message. Tell me your iPhone model and iOS version if something is broken.

Questions

Can I see a photo before the roll comes back?

No. That's the app.

Can I delete one bad photo?

No. You can delete a whole roll, and that can't be undone.

Can I change the return date?

No, it's set when you load the film. A roll never comes back before the first 09:00 at least 8 hours after it is sent. If you send a roll after its date has passed, or too close to it, the app offers the earliest time or a later one.

What does film cost?

Nothing. 24-PHOTO ROLL and 36-PHOTO ROLL are free and unlimited. One optional purchase, B&W, 1:1 and Custom look in Settings, adds black & white, square photos and a custom film look. You pay once and keep it for good.

I sent a roll early. Do I get the unshot photos back?

No. Unshot film isn't refunded, just like a real camera.

My purchase didn't add black & white, square and Custom look.

Open Settings, tap B&W, 1:1 and Custom look, then Restore purchase, and make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used.

I want a refund.

Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. Where are my rolls?

Rolls and photos live on the phone that shot them. They move to a new phone only through an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer). Your B&W, 1:1 and Custom look purchase follows your Apple Account; use Restore purchase. Export developed rolls to Photos to keep them safe.

I deleted the app.

Deleting the app deletes every roll on that phone, including rolls still at the lab.

Export doesn't work.

Export needs access to Photos. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → 24×36.

I never get a notification when a roll is back.

Go to Settings → Notifications → 24×36 and allow notifications.
