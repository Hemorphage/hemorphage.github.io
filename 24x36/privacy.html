Privacy

24×36 collects nothing. Last updated 14 October 2026.

No account, no server

There is no account, no login, and no server we control. Nothing you shoot is uploaded anywhere.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no third-party code that phones home. We collect no personal data.

Your photos

Photos are stored on your device only. They are included in your device backup but don't sync between devices. They leave the app only when you export a roll to Photos. Location is never recorded.

Camera and Photos access

The camera is used to take pictures. Photos access is used only to create an album when you export a roll.

Purchases

The only purchase is the optional B&W, 1:1 and Custom look. It is handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details. Restoring it uses your Apple Account. See Apple's privacy policy.

Feedback

If you choose Send feedback, your mail app opens with the app version, iOS version and device model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Notifications

Notifications are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
